oxygenpad
about

An optimized, lightweight and sleek-looking FOSS (Free and Open Source Software) alternative to Soundpad written in C++.

It features a sleek and modern UI, fast sound engine and a simple architecture that allows for seamless updates.

how it works

Oxygenpad uses VB-AUDIO Voicemeeter virtual "microphone" and "speakers" to play desired sounds, mix them with you real microphone and output it as audio stream into virtual "microphone" device made by VB-AUDIO Voicemeeter.
